# W3C Community Group proposal

Submitted 2026-11-26; awaiting W3C staff moderation, then 5 supporters to launch.

**Name:** Open Mobile Document Agent Community Group · **Short name:** `omda`

The mission of the Open Mobile Document Agent Community Group is to develop an open, model-agnostic way for personal devices to read documents their owners hold and to state, verifiably, what those documents prove. As phones gain on-device AI, the group aims to ensure that every extracted fact can be traced to its source on the page, checked by deterministic means where possible, kept under the owner's control, and shared with apps, assistants and verifiers only to the extent the owner chooses.

The group will build on the Open Mobile Document Agent (OMDA) v0.1 draft (https://openmobiledocumentagent.org) and reuse existing standards wherever they apply, including W3C Verifiable Credentials 2.0, SD-JWT and ICAO Doc 9303. Anticipated deliverables include:

- a data model for evidenced assertions, with provenance and document lineage;
- verification semantics and an open registry of deterministic checks;
- domain profiles, starting with identity documents;
- an agent interface aligned with the Model Context Protocol, Android AppFunctions and iOS App Intents;
- a selective-disclosure profile of Verifiable Credentials for document-derived claims;
- a synthetic conformance test suite.

The group will not define document formats, extraction algorithms or AI models, will not certify implementations, and will coordinate with the Verifiable Credentials Working Group and the Credentials Community Group to avoid overlapping work. Participation is welcome from implementers of on-device AI, wallet and identity specialists, document-processing practitioners and privacy experts. Specifications produced are Community Group Reports, not W3C Standards.
